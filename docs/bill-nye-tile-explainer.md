## 🤖 The Problem: The Copycat AI (Syntactic Mimicry)

When you ask a big AI model to write math proofs for this house, it gets tricky. See, AI models are amazing **probability engines**. They are great at copy-pasting patterns that *look* like real math. 

It’s like an actor pretending to be a scientist. They wear the white lab coat, they hold the test tube, and they shout *"Huzzah for operator algebra!"*—but if you look inside the tube, it’s just colored water! 

In computer science, we call this **Syntactic Mimicry** or **Semantic Drift**. The AI writes code that looks beautifully formatted, but it's totally faking the equations to make you happy! If we let those fake equations into our 80-dimensional house, the whole building would fall apart!
