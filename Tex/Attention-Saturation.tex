\begin{abstract}
This paper investigates attention saturation and dimensional collapse—systemic mathematical vulnerabilities in transformer-based autonomous substrates where \texttt{Softmax} operations degrade high-dimensional context representations under operational stress \citep{vaswani2017attention, dong2021attention}. We demonstrate that boundary constraints can force attention entropy to collapse, compromising systemic compliance with International Humanitarian Law (IHL). Upstream Query-Key normalization and deterministic neurosymbolic sandboxing are proposed as a verifiable safety envelope.
\end{abstract}
