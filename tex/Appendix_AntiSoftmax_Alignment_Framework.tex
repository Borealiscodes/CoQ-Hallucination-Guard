\subsection{A.1 Motivation: Softmax as a Collapse Operator}

The classical softmax operator
\[
\mathrm{softmax}(x_i) = \frac{e^{x_i}}{\sum_j e^{x_j}}
\]
acts as a collapse map
\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{soft}} : M \rightarrow \Delta^{n-1}
\]
from a geometric manifold $M$ into the probability simplex $\Delta^{n-1}$.
This mapping destroys geometric structure, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item origin information,
    \item curvature,
    \item holonomy,
    \item slack dimensions,
    \item spectral energy distribution.
\end{itemize}

Softmax is therefore incompatible with systems requiring reversible operators,
stable provenance, ternary projection, NDH (Non-Dual Holonomy), or FSFE manifold invariants.

Introduce slack coordinates $s \in \mathbb{R}^k$ such that:
\[

\[
\mathrm{softmax}(\mathrm{Hol}(v)) \neq \mathrm{Hol}(\mathrm{softmax}(v)).

\paragraph{Proof Sketch.}

Thus softmax is incompatible with NDH and any holonomy-based operator.
