\section{Appendix D: Spectral Anti-Drift Bounds}
\label{appendix:spectralantidrift}

\subsection{D.1 Motivation}

Spectral drift occurs when transport across collapse surfaces accumulates
energy in a way that destabilizes projection thresholds, curvature monotonicity,
or NDH invariants. Classical softmax-based systems exhibit uncontrolled spectral
drift due to collapse-induced energy concentration. Under the FSFE manifold,
spectral drift becomes measurable and bounded.

\subsection{D.2 Spectral Energy Definition}

Let $\sigma : M \rightarrow \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ be the spectral energy function
of the FSFE manifold. For any $v \in M$:
\[
\mathrm{SpecInt}(v) = \int_{\gamma_v} \sigma(\gamma(t)) \, dt
\]
where $\gamma_v$ is a transport path through $v$.

\paragraph{Slack Stability.}
\[
\mathrm{SpecInt}(v + s) = \mathrm{SpecInt}(v)
\]
for slack vectors $s \in \mathbb{R}^k$.

\paragraph{Holonomy Invariance.}
\[
\mathrm{SpecInt}(\mathrm{Hol}_\gamma(v)) = \mathrm{SpecInt}(v).
\]

\subsection{D.3 Collapse-Surface Spectral Bounds}

Let $C_k$ be a collapse surface from Appendix C. Define the spectral bound:
\[
B_k = \sup_{v \in C_k} \sigma(v).
\]

Collapse surfaces satisfy:

\paragraph{Curvature Monotonicity.}
\[
k_1 \le k_2 \Rightarrow B_{k_1} \le B_{k_2}.
\]

\paragraph{Boundary Stability.}
For $v \in \partial C_k$:
\[
\sigma(v) \le B_k.
\]

\subsection{D.4 Anti-Drift Condition}

Transport along collapse surfaces must satisfy:
\[
\mathrm{SpecInt}(T_C(v)) \le \mathrm{SpecInt}(v).
\]

This ensures spectral energy does not accumulate under collapse transport.

\paragraph{Metric Preservation.}
Since $g(T_C(v), T_C(v)) = g(v,v)$, transport does not introduce new spectral
modes.

\paragraph{Curvature Preservation.}
\[
\kappa(T_C(v)) = \kappa(v)
\]
ensures spectral bounds remain valid.

\paragraph{Slack Preservation.}
Slack coordinates prevent collapse-induced energy concentration.

\subsection{D.5 Anti-Drift Operator}

Define the anti-drift operator:
\[
A_{\mathrm{drift}} : M \rightarrow M
\]
with:
\[
A_{\mathrm{drift}}(v) = v - \epsilon_\sigma
\]
where $\epsilon_\sigma$ is a spectral correction term satisfying:

\begin{itemize}
    \item $\sigma(v - \epsilon_\sigma) \le \sigma(v)$,
    \item $\epsilon_\sigma$ lies in slack dimensions,
    \item $\kappa(v - \epsilon_\sigma) = \kappa(v)$,
    \item $\mathrm{Hol}_\gamma(v - \epsilon_\sigma) = \mathrm{Hol}_\gamma(v)$.
\end{itemize}

Thus $A_{\mathrm{drift}}$ is FSFE-compliant.
