\subsection{Jacobian Rank Deficiency and Volume Vanishing}
To track the geometric evolution of the context volume form under state reduction, we evaluate the Jacobian matrix $J(\sigma) \in \R^{n \times n}$. The components of the Jacobian are given by:
\begin{equation}
    J_{ij} = \frac{\partial \sigma_i}{\partial v_j} = \sigma_i (\delta_{ij} - \sigma_j)
\end{equation}
where $\delta_{ij}$ is the Kronecker delta.

\begin{theorem}[Degenerate Simplex Collapse]
Let $\omega$ be the high-dimensional volume form on $\M$. Under the projection $\sigma$, the pulled-back volume form $\omegaSigma = \sigma^* \omega$ vanishes identically everywhere on the ambient space:
\begin{equation}
    \omegaSigma = 0
\end{equation}
induced by a strict, invariant Jacobian rank deficiency.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $\bm{1} = [1, 1, \dots, 1]^T$ be the all-ones vector. We evaluate the right-multiplication of the Jacobian matrix by $\bm{1}$:
\begin{equation}
    (J \bm{1})_i = \sum_{j=1}^n J_{ij} = \sum_{j=1}^n \sigma_i (\delta_{ij} - \sigma_j)
\end{equation}
Expanding the summation:
\begin{equation}
    (J \bm{1})_i = \sigma_i \sum_{j=1}^n \delta_{ij} - \sigma_i \sum_{j=1}^n \sigma_j
\end{equation}
Since $\sum_{j=1}^n \delta_{ij} = 1$ and the output vector is constrained to the simplex such that $\sum_{j=1}^n \sigma_j = 1$, the expression reduces to:
\begin{equation}
    (J \bm{1})_i = \sigma_i (1) - \sigma_i (1) = 0
\end{equation}
Because $J \bm{1} = \bm{0}$ for all valid logit profiles, the vector $\bm{1}$ lies permanently within the null space of the Jacobian ($\bm{1} \in \ker(J)$). Consequently, $\text{rank}(J) \leq n - 1$. 

The wedge product defining the top-dimensional volume form requires linear independence across all coordinate differentials:
\begin{equation}
    \omegaSigma = \det(J) \, dv_1 \wedge dv_2 \wedge \dots \wedge dv_n
\end{equation}
Since $\text{rank}(J) < n$, the determinant $\det(J) = 0$ identically, forcing $\omegaSigma = 0$. The geometric phase-space volume collapses into a zero-volume boundary projection, erasing continuous historical path holonomy.
\end{proof}
